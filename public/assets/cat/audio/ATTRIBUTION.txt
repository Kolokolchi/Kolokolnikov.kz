Cat meow sound effects (replaced 2026-11-10)

All five recordings are real cats, published on Freesound under CC0 1.0 Universal
(public-domain dedication): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Files were made from the public HQ previews (128 kbps MP3) of each sound.

Common processing: 110 Hz high-pass, trimmed to a single meow, 8 ms fade-in and
70 ms fade-out, loudness matched to -18 LUFS (peaks below -5 dBFS), mono 44.1 kHz MP3, 96 kbps.

meow-1.mp3 - an ordinary adult meow
Original: "Cat Meowing" by lextrack (Freesound #333916), "meow of a cat"
Source page: https://freesound.org/people/lextrack/sounds/333916/
Trimmed to 0.07-1.24 s.

meow-2.mp3 - a short meow
Original: "cat meow short" by skymary (Freesound #412017), "a cat meowing (short)"
Source page: https://freesound.org/people/skymary/sounds/412017/
Trimmed to 0.24-1.15 s.

meow-3.mp3 - an insistent meow
Original: "New Cat Meow 1" by steffcaffrey (Freesound #479272), "My cat's very annoying meow. Zoom H4N"
Source page: https://freesound.org/people/steffcaffrey/sounds/479272/
Whole recording (0.00-0.92 s).

meow-4.mp3 - a kitten
Original: "Cute Kitten Meow" by Breviceps (Freesound #448084), "A cute kitten meow"
Source page: https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/448084/
Whole recording (0.00-0.60 s).

meow-5.mp3 - a hungry cat (kept from the previous set: the only natural one there)
Original: "cat meow" by tuberatanka (Freesound #110011), "my hungry cat meowing"
Source page: https://freesound.org/people/tuberatanka/sounds/110011/
Trimmed to 0.10-1.50 s.

The previous meow-1..4 (Kerzoven, OpenGameArt "cat purr meow") were dropped:
noisy, muffled, with long silences and odd chirps.
